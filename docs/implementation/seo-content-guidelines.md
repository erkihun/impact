# SEO content guidelines for editors

These are the rules the CMS checks. "Blocking" issues stop publication; warnings are shown in the SEO centre and the page editor.

## Titles

- Write a specific, human title. The site adds "| Impact Consulting" once; do not type it yourself.
- Aim for 30–60 characters including the suffix. Longer titles keep their descriptive part and drop the suffix.
- Every page needs a unique title. Avoid generic titles such as "Home", "Services" or "Untitled".
- Case studies: lead with the outcome ("Cutting permit processing time by 40%"), not the client's name.

## Descriptions

- One or two sentences, 50–160 characters, that tell a searcher what the page offers.
- If the SEO description is empty, the summary or excerpt is used, so keep summaries clear.
- Do not repeat the same description across pages and do not list keywords.

## URLs (slugs)

- Lowercase words separated by single hyphens, e.g. `digital-transformation`. The CMS normalizes what you type.
- Keep slugs short and stable. Reserved words (`admin`, `search`, `sitemap`, `en`, `am`, …) are rejected.
- Changing the slug of a published page creates a permanent redirect from the old URL automatically. Draft slug changes do not affect the live page.

## Headings

- Each page has exactly one main heading (`h1`): the page title or the composition's page-header section. Do not add a second page-header section.
- Use section headings in order (H2, then H3). Headings describe structure, not styling.

## Images

- Every meaningful image needs alternative text describing its content or purpose. Decorative images are marked decorative instead.
- Upload high-quality sources: the site serves resized WebP versions (480, 960 and 1600 px wide) with dimensions, never the original file.
- Social images work best at 1200 × 630 px, JPEG or PNG.

## Internal links

- Link related services, industries, experts, case studies and insights in the page editor's "Related content". Only published pages can be linked, and links appear automatically on both pages where relevant.
- A page with no related links is reported as an orphan risk.
- Use descriptive link text; never "click here".

## Indexing controls

- Leave robots on "Default" for normal pages. Use `noindex` only for thin or temporary pages that should stay reachable.
- Use the canonical override only when a page duplicates another page on this site.
- Previews, drafts, internal search and admin pages are never indexed regardless of these settings.

## Geography and claims

- Select geographic relevance (Ethiopia, Addis Ababa, East Africa, Africa, International) only when the content genuinely addresses that market. The site never adds a location to titles automatically.
- Do not publish ratings, reviews, awards or client claims without written approval and evidence. Structured data never contains ratings, reviews or awards.

## What not to do

No keyword stuffing, hidden text, doorway or city pages without real content, AI filler pages, or content written for search engines rather than readers. `meta keywords` is not used.
