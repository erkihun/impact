## Review findings

- The home page retains the strongest approved Executive Editorial composition.
- About now matches the paper/editorial language instead of presenting a generic card row.
- Search uses the knowledge variant, a coherent numbered result rhythm, and clear recovery actions.
- Event and vacancy headers align with the shared editorial variants.
- The secure file control is legible, restrained, and responsive at 375 px.
- Footer, consent, legal navigation, and calls to action remain consistent across routes.
- English and Amharic layouts remain readable without clipping or horizontal overflow.

## Brand restraint

Gold is reserved for markers and selective actions. Teal identifies advisory actions. Knowledge blue identifies research/information contexts. Navy anchors institutional hierarchy. Pages use editorial rules and whitespace instead of decorative card walls or gradients.
